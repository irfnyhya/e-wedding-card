Questions couples ask

How do I make a card?

Tap Create a card, pick a design and fill in the steps: names, majlis details and the extras you want. The phone preview shows exactly what guests will see. Your draft saves as you go.

How do I pay?

Once with FPX online banking through ToyyibPay, from any Malaysian bank. No subscription and no hidden fees. Your card goes live the moment the payment is approved.

Which package do I need?

The package sets itself from the features you turn on, and the price shows before you pay. Upgrade later and pay only the difference.

How long does my card stay online?

One year from payment on every package, and you can edit it any time during that year.

Can I change details after paying?

Yes. Open My cards, choose Edit and save. Guests see the change the next time they open the card.

How do I send the card to guests?

Copy your link or tap Share on WhatsApp right after paying; the link is always in My cards too. With Permata and up, each guest can get a personal link with their name on the cover.

Where do I see RSVPs and ucapan?

In My cards. Replies and wishes appear as soon as guests send them, with the total pax attending. Diraja adds a CSV export and WhatsApp reminders.

Do guests need to install anything?

No. The card opens in any phone browser, straight from WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram.

Can we have two cards, for nikah and bertandang?

Yes. Each card has its own link, date, venue and RSVP list, and is paid separately.
