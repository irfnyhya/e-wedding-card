Questions couples ask

Can I change details after paying?

Yes. Open My cards, choose Edit, and save. Guests see the new details on their next visit. Changing to a bigger package charges only the difference.

Do guests need to install anything?

No. The card opens in any phone browser from WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram.

Can we have two cards, for nikah and bertandang?

Yes. Create a second card with its own date and venue. Each card has its own RSVP list.

How do personal guest links work?

On the Diraja package, type your guest names in the dashboard. Each one gets a link that puts their name on the cover.
