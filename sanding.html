Soalan

Questions couples ask

Can I change details after paying?

Yes. Open My cards, choose Edit and save. Guests see the change the next time they open the card.

Do guests need to install anything?

No. The card opens in any phone browser, straight from WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram.

Can we have two cards, for nikah and bertandang?

Yes. Each card has its own date, venue and RSVP list.

Where do the flowers come from?

They are hand-coloured botanical plates by Pierre-Joseph Redouté and his contemporaries, printed in the early 1800s and now in the public domain.

SandingPrototype build. Payments are simulated. Redouté botanical plates, Cuvilliés rococo frames, old maps of Malaya and Malayan postcards (c.1801–1930) are public domain, via the Cleveland Museum of Art and Wikimedia Commons.
